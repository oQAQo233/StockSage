Washington to review Northwind-Sunbelt merger

Regulators at the Department of Transportation said they would review the $2.4 billion merger between Northwind Airlines and Sunbelt Air, a deal that would reshape domestic air travel.

The Department of Transportation is based in Washington. Officials said the review would focus on routes where the two carriers overlap.

Northwind Airlines is based in Chicago and Sunbelt Air is based in Atlanta. Together the carriers would control 18 percent of domestic capacity.

Sunbelt Air has signed a deal with its pilots union that guarantees no furloughs for 18 months, according to a filing. Northwind Airlines declined to comment.
