# Padel’s Olympic ambition

Padel is the fastest growing racket sport in the world, and is pushing to make its Olympic debut at the Brisbane Games in 2032. Al Jazeera’s Tom Sizeland was at the Padel World Cup in Doha, where the world’s best players are competing for gold.
