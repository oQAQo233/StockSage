# Port of Los Angeles strike ends with new contract

Dockworkers at the Port of Los Angeles have signed a deal with the Pacific Maritime Association, ending a three-week strike that had halted cargo handling.

The Pacific Maritime Association is based in San Francisco. Union leaders said the agreement included a 15 percent wage increase over four years.

Retailers had warned that a longer stoppage would push up prices before the holiday season.
