# 3,000 tarantulas hidden inside luggage seized at Peruvian airport

In a peculiar failed smuggling attempt, 3,000 tarantulas were seized at a Peruvian airport, after an anonymous tip led authorities to search the suitcases of two German nationals and a Polish national boarding a flight to Germany.
