# Indonesia rushes thousands of troops to Jakarta amid wiretap scandal

Indonesia is deploying thousands of troops in Jakarta amid an alleged wiretapping scandal targeting President Prabowo Subianto, ministers and others. Among those implicated is Gen Listyo Sigit Prabowo, who was appointed presidential chief of staff just last month.
