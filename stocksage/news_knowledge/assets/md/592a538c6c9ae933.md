# ECB raises rates again as inflation stays above target

The European Central Bank raised interest rates by 25 basis points on Thursday, its third increase this year, as inflation stayed above target across the euro area.

Christine Lagarde, president of the European Central Bank, said the decision was unanimous and warned that further moves would depend on incoming data.

The European Central Bank is based in Frankfurt. Officials at Nordbank said markets had priced in the move, while analysts at Rhine Capital argued that the cycle was close to its end.

Since March, policymakers have raised rates at every meeting. Consumer prices in the euro area rose 3.1 percent in October, down from 3.4 percent a month earlier.
