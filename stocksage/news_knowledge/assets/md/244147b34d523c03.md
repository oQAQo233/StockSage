# TSMC expands Arizona plants as demand for advanced chips grows

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing has invested in Arizona, where it plans to build three fabrication plants by 2028.

The company said the investment would total $65 billion. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing is based in Hsinchu.

Officials in Phoenix said the plants would employ 6,000 workers. Analysts at Pacific Research argued that the expansion would reduce reliance on Taiwanese capacity.
