# Police bodycam captures moments before deadly Russian strike in Ukraine

Bodycam footage released by Ukraine’s National Police shows people rushing into an underground shelter as Russian bombs approach. A November 2 attack on the site, which was being used as a shelter and aid distribution point, killed at least 40 people, with dozens still missing.
