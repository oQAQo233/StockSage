# Northwind Airlines to buy Sunbelt Air in $2.4 billion deal

Northwind Airlines has acquired Sunbelt Air in a $2.4 billion deal, the two carriers announced on Monday, creating the third-largest airline in the country.

Northwind Airlines is based in Chicago. Maria Chen, chief executive of Northwind Airlines, said the combination would add 40 routes and keep both brands for at least a year.

Sunbelt Air is based in Atlanta and employs 12,000 people. The deal is expected to close in the first quarter, subject to approval by the Department of Transportation.
