# U.S. bans chip equipment exports to Chinese firms

The United States has banned chip exports to several Chinese firms, the Department of Commerce announced on Tuesday.

The Department of Commerce said the rule covers lithography tools and would take effect in January. ASML Holding said it was assessing the impact on its order book.

ASML Holding is based in Veldhoven. Shares in the company fell 4 percent in Amsterdam.
