OPEC cuts oil output by 1.2 million barrels a day

OPEC has cut oil production by 1.2 million barrels a day, the group announced on Sunday, in a bid to support prices.

Saudi Arabia has cut output by 500,000 barrels a day, according to a statement. OPEC is based in Vienna.

Analysts at Meridian Energy said the cuts would tighten the market in the first half of next year. Brent crude rose 2 percent on Monday.
